# Orbit privacy policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Orbit does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data. It never connects to the internet: it has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no update checks. Your files are processed entirely on your PC and are never uploaded anywhere.

## What it reads on your PC, and why

| What | Why | Kept? |
|---|---|---|
| The files you select in File Explorer, only when you press Alt + Q or choose Orbit from the right-click menu | To show the right actions and run the one you pick | No. Results are saved next to your original files; nothing else is kept. |
| Thumbnails Windows has already cached for those files | To show a preview in the centre of the ring | No |
| The contents of files you choose to convert | To convert, compress, resize, merge or rename them, which is what you asked for | No. The originals are never overwritten. |

When you convert Word or PowerPoint files, Orbit asks Microsoft Office (or LibreOffice) on your PC to do the conversion. When a video format needs it, it may use ffmpeg if you have installed it. These all run locally.

## What it saves

A record of your last batch rename, so it can be undone, and any plugins you install yourself. Both stay on your PC in Orbit's app data folder. Uninstalling Orbit removes them.
